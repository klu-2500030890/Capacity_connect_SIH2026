# CAPACITY CONNECT — 2-Minute Pitch Deck

---

### Slide 1: The Problem
> **"Most LMS platforms are digital graveyards of mandatory compliance videos."**

- **The Broken Status Quo**: Organizations buy course platforms, but employees don't know what skills they actually lack, managers can't see who is truly proficient, and peer knowledge remains locked in Slack threads.
- **The Core Mandate**: True capacity building requires three pillars functioning together: **Training Delivery**, **Competency Development**, and **Peer Knowledge Sharing**.

---

### Slide 2: The Solution — CAPACITY CONNECT
> **"An Enterprise Digital Capacity Building & Learning Management Portal"**

1. **Precision Competency Engine**: Real-time Level 1–5 skill mapping. When an engineer passes an assessment, their Skill Radar levels up instantly.
2. **AI Skill Gap Advisor**: Bridges career transitions by comparing live user competencies against target roles and auto-generating focused curricula.
3. **Peer Knowledge Sharing**: Employees author wiki articles, upvote solutions, and route hard questions to Level 4+ verified domain experts.

---

### Slide 3: Live Role-Based Workspaces (RBAC)

- **Learner OS (`/learner`)**: Personal Skill Radar, gamified streaks/XP, modular course player, and verifiable PDF certificates.
- **People Manager Hub (`/manager`)**: Team-wide competency heatmap, at-risk watchlist, and a **1-Click Manager Nudge** system.
- **L&D Trainer Studio (`/trainer`)**: Modular curriculum builder, cohort completion analytics, and **AI Quiz Synthesizer from raw markdown**.
- **Super Admin Command (`/admin`)**: Master competency framework governance, org-wide heatmap, and training ROI metrics.

---

### Slide 5: Business Impact & ROI Framing

- **3.8x Training ROI**: Eliminates redundant course purchases by targeting exact skill deficiencies.
- **60% Faster Onboarding**: Structured role paths accelerate transition from Junior to Senior engineers in weeks instead of months.
- **Zero Knowledge Loss**: Peer-to-peer wiki captures incident post-mortems and architectural decisions internally.
